What I Do

I support the full grant lifecycle, from proposal preparation and submission through award setup, fiscal management, and compliance.

  • Prepare and submit research proposals, including budgets, budget justifications, biosketches, and sponsor and university forms.
  • Set up awards and manage post-award activity, including budgets, modifications, annual reports, expenditure review, and compliance.
  • Monitor balances, reconcile 40 research accounts monthly, and provide financial reports to Principal Investigators.
  • Coordinate travel, reimbursements, purchasing, events, and other research support.
  • Collaborate with faculty, research teams, and the Texas Robotics Program Manager on special projects.

I use AI, Smartsheet, and Python to streamline:

  • Award risk scoring and PI effort tracking across active grants.
  • GRA roster and costing reconciliation, Data Shuttle uploads, and data corrections.
  • Finance, HR, procurement, and account setup workflows.
  • Payment processing, including VP5, VPE, and VP2 packet assembly, PDF stamping, and email triage.
  • Process documentation and training so teammates can use the tools independently.
